What am I looking at?

The explorer follows scored phenophases on a fixed roster of tagged plants, with cadence, coverage, and interval-censored onset kept visible. Those observations describe monitored timing—not plant abundance, productivity, unobserved phenophases, or what caused a seasonal change.
