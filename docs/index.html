Know what the records can carry.

NEON crews repeatedly score named phenophases on fixed, tagged plants. That design is powerful for timing—and easy to overread if support, censoring, and missing observations disappear.

This explorer can

  • Show when recorded phenophases were active among scored plant-year-week opportunities.
  • Compare plant-year onset timing after repeated visits are collapsed correctly.
  • Expose cadence, sample size, green-up coverage, and left-censored records.
  • Follow the same species across sites as an observational space-for-time view.

This explorer cannot

  • Estimate plant abundance, productivity, survival, or demographic performance.
  • Treat an unscored phenophase as a biological absence.
  • Turn repeated visits into independent biological replicates.
  • Identify temperature or climate as the cause of a timing pattern.

Suite role · evidence hinge

The plant-timing lens in the Driver Cascade.

Phenology is the suite’s bridge from environmental timing to seasonal biological response. Its corrected plant-year signals are candidates for Cascade context, but they do not become causal Driver evidence simply because the charts align.
